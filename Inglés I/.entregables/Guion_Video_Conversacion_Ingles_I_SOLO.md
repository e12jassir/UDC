## 🗣️ Guion Individual (Tú Solo a Cámara con Fotos)

**Esteban:** Hey everyone! Welcome to my neighborhood presentation for English I. Today, I want to show you some photos and introduce four awesome people who live around here.

---

📸 *(MOSTRAR FOTO 1: Carlos el Panadero)*  
**Esteban:** First, look at this photo. This is Carlos Mendoza. He is 35 years old. Is he a doctor? No, he isn't! He is actually our local baker. He is from right here, Cartagena, and his bread is honestly amazing.

---

📸 *(MOSTRAR FOTO 2: Mariana la Fotógrafa)*  
**Esteban:** Now check this out — see this woman with the camera? Her name is Mariana Gómez. She is 28 years old. Is she a tourist? No, she isn't! She is a professional photographer. Where is she from? She is Mexican, from Guadalajara, but she is here now for work.

---

📸 *(MOSTRAR FOTO 3: Mateo y Lucas los Programadores)*  
**Esteban:** And look at these two guys by the bench. Their names are Mateo and Lucas Rossi. They are 24 years old. Are they Spanish? Nope, they aren't Spanish! They are Italian. What is their job? They are software developers, and their work is fully remote.

---

📸 *(MOSTRAR FOTO 4: Elena la Profesora/Estudiante)*  
**Esteban:** Finally, what about this woman over here? Is she a doctor or something? No, she isn't a doctor yet! Her name is Elena Torres. She is 21 years old. She is an English teacher and also a university student.

---

**Esteban:** As you can see, our neighborhood is full of interesting and talented people! Thank you so much for watching. Have a great day, bye!
