## 📝 English Text (Ready to Submit)

On Saturday mornings, I usually visit Bazurto Market, a lively traditional market located near the center of Cartagena. There are dozens of colorful stalls selling fresh tropical fruits, local vegetables, and seafood. I always see vendors preparing traditional fried snacks, fresh bread, and cold juices like lemonade and passion fruit. The entire place smells delicious, and local music plays in the background while people shop. During my visit, I buy two sweet pineapples and a hot cup of black coffee from a friendly vendor. There is also a small bakery corner where customers wait in line for warm cheese bread. I really enjoy walking through this market because the ingredients are fresh and the vendors are always welcoming.
