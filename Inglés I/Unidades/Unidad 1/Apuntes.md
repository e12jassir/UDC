## 🧭 Unit Overview

This unit focuses on **describing people, jobs, and places** using foundational grammar structures. By the end of the unit, you should be able to introduce people, describe your neighborhood, and ask and answer basic questions in English.

**Core Topics:**
- Present Simple and Present Continuous
- Verb *to be* (affirmative, negative, interrogative)
- Describing people (appearance, personality)
- Describing places (neighborhood, community)
- Wh- Questions
- Vocabulary: Jobs, nationalities, community places

## 1. Verb *To Be* — Complete Grammar

### 1.1 Affirmative

| Subject | Full Form | Contracted |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| I | I am | I'm |
| You | You are | You're |
| He / She / It | He/She/It is | He's / She's / It's |
| We | We are | We're |
| They | They are | They're |

**Examples in context:**
```
I'm a software engineering student.
She's from Barranquilla, Colombia.
They're my classmates at UDC.
```

### 1.2 Negative

| Subject | Full Form | Contracted |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| I | I am not | I'm not |
| You | You are not | You aren't |
| He / She / It | He is not | He isn't |
| We | We are not | We aren't |
| They | They are not | They aren't |

```
I'm not a doctor — I'm a student.
He isn't from Bogotá; he's from Medellín.
```

### 1.3 Interrogative

| Type | Structure | Example |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Yes/No | `Are you…?` / `Is he…?` | Are you a teacher? |
| Short Answer | `Yes, I am.` / `No, I'm not.` | Yes, I am. / No, I'm not. |
| Wh- Question | `What is…?` / `Where are…?` | Where are they from? |

## 2. Present Simple

### 2.1 Affirmative

| Subject | Rule | Example |
|:---:|:---|:---|
| I / You / We / They | Base verb | I **work** in a hospital |
| He / She / It | Add **-s** or **-es** | She **works** in a hospital |

**Third person singular — Spelling rules:**

| Verb ending | Rule | Example |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Most verbs | + **s** | play → play**s** |
| -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -z | + **es** | watch → watch**es** |
| Consonant + -y | -y → **ies** | study → stud**ies** |
| -o | + **es** | go → go**es** |
| Irregular | — | have → **has**, be → **is** |

### 2.2 Negative

| Subject | Structure |
|:---:|:---|
| I / You / We / They | Subject + **don't** + base verb |
| He / She / It | Subject + **doesn't** + base verb |

```
I don't live in Bogotá.
He doesn't work on weekends.
They don't speak French.
```

### 2.3 Interrogative (Yes/No)

```
Do you study at UDC?   → Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
Does she live nearby?  → Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.
```

### 2.4 Wh- Questions in Present Simple

| Question Word | Asks About | Example |
|:---:|:---|:---|
| **What** | Thing / action | What do you do? |
| **Where** | Place | Where do they live? |
| **Who** | Person | Who does she work with? |
| **When** | Time | When does class start? |
| **Why** | Reason | Why do you study English? |
| **How** | Manner / degree | How do you get to school? |
| **How often** | Frequency | How often do you exercise? |

> ⚠️ **Error común de hispanohablantes:** Usar doble sujeto: ~~"Where does she lives?"~~ → Correcto: "Where does she **live**?" (con *does*, el verbo va en forma base).

## 3. Present Continuous

### 3.1 Structure

`Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing`

### 3.2 Spelling Rules for *-ing*

| Verb Type | Rule | Example |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Most verbs | + **ing** | read → read**ing** |
| Silent -e | Drop e + **ing** | make → mak**ing** |
| Short vowel + consonant | Double consonant + **ing** | run → run**ning**, sit → sit**ting** |
| -ie | -ie → **ying** | lie → ly**ing** |

### 3.3 When to Use Each Tense

| Present Simple | Present Continuous |
|:---|:---|
| Habitual actions / routines | Actions happening **right now** |
| General truths / facts | Temporary situations |
| Scheduled events (future) | Developing situations |
| States (know, like, want) | (States usually don't use continuous) |

```
I study English every Wednesday.     (hábito — Simple)
I am studying English right now.     (acción actual — Continuous)

She lives in Barranquilla.           (fact — Simple)
She is living with her aunt this month. (temporal — Continuous)
```

> ⚠️ **Stative verbs** (verbos de estado) no se usan normalmente en continuo:  
> like, love, hate, want, need, know, understand, believe, have (posesión), seem, appear.
>
> ~~"I am knowing the answer."~~ → ✅ "I **know** the answer."

## 5. Describing Your Neighborhood — Writing Model

**Task type:** Describe 5-8 people or places in your neighborhood using Present Simple and *to be*.

**Model text:**
> My neighborhood is called El Prado. It is a quiet and friendly community in Barranquilla. There is a park near my house where children play every afternoon. My neighbor, Mrs. García, is a retired teacher. She is very kind and she always waves hello. Across the street, there is a small bakery. The baker, Mr. Pérez, works from 5 a.m. to 2 p.m. every day. I love my neighborhood because it is safe and clean.

**Useful connectors:**
```
also, in addition, however, near, across from, next to, 
in the morning / afternoon / evening, every day, usually, always
```

## 6. Errores Comunes de Hispanohablantes

| Error | Incorrecto | Correcto | Explicación |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Omitir sujeto | *"Is a good student"* | *"He is a good student"* | El inglés requiere sujeto explícito siempre |
| Olvidar -s 3ª persona | *"She work"* | *"She works"* | Con he/she/it, agregar -s/-es |
| Usar *is* con plurales | *"They is"* | *"They are"* | Solo singular lleva *is* |
| Doble negación | *"He doesn't knows"* | *"He doesn't know"* | Con *does*, el verbo va en base |
| ¿Qué? = What, no Which | *"Which is your name?"* | *"What is your name?"* | *What* para preguntas abiertas en contexto de identidad |
| Verbo de estado en continuo | *"I am knowing"* | *"I know"* | *Know* es estativo, no admite -ing |
| Traducción literal de "tengo" | *"I have 25 years"* | *"I am 25 years old"* | La edad en inglés usa *to be*, no *to have* |

## 9. Quick Reference

```
VERB TO BE:
I am / I'm          We are / We're
You are / You're    They are / They're
He/She/It is / He's

PRESENT SIMPLE NEGATION:
I/You/We/They + DON'T + base verb
He/She/It    + DOESN'T + base verb

PRESENT CONTINUOUS:
am/is/are + verb-ING

WH- QUESTIONS:
What + do/does + subject + base verb + ?
Where does she live?
What do you do?

DESCRIBING PEOPLE:
Subject + is/are + age + build + hair description
"She is a young, slim woman with long, dark hair."
```
